# Internship at Carl Zeiss GOM Metrology GmbH

**Department:** AI & Algorithms X-Ray (IQS)
**Location:** Braunschweig, Germany
**Duration:** 3 months

## About the Internship

This internship gives me practical industry experience in industrial X-ray computed tomography (CT)
and metrology, alongside my MSc in Mathematics at the University of Bremen.

## Focus Areas

- Analysing real industrial CT scan datasets
- Studying object geometry in X-ray / CT imaging
- Identifying and investigating sources of geometric error in CT measurements
- Exploring numerical methods to reduce geometric errors
- Connecting mathematical modelling and scientific computing with real-world metrology

## Skills Applied

- Projective geometry and CT imaging geometry
- Numerical analysis and optimisation
- Python for scientific computing (NumPy, Matplotlib, scikit-image)
- Data analysis and visualisation

## Related Work

- **Epipolar Consistency in Transmission Imaging** – my Modeling Project at the University of Bremen,
  in collaboration with Carl Zeiss GOM Metrology GmbH.
